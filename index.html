The problem

International trade information is spread across invoices, transport documents, origin evidence and technical descriptions. Importers and their advisers repeat work, reconcile missing facts and explain decisions across disconnected tools.

GoodsPass brings that preparation into one workflow. We aim to reduce repeated data entry and preserve the reasoning behind each dossier, not to remove legal responsibilities or promise error-free automation.

02 / 09
GoodsPass

How the product works

Upload commercial and transport documents → review extracted product facts → consider classification candidates and origin → evaluate valuation, tariffs and conditional obligations → review and retain the dossier.

The workflow is designed for importers, customs professionals and logistics teams. Explanations and source references accompany decisions; missing facts remain visible.
